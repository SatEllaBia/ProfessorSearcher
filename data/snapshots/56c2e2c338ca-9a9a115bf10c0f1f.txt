## About
Dr. Ruibang Luo is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Hong Kong. He completed his PhD training in Bioinformatics at the University of Hong Kong with Dr. Tak-Wah Lam (2010–2015), and his postdoctoral training at the Center of Computational Biology, Johns Hopkins University with Drs. Steven Salzberg and Michael Schatz (2016–2017). Luo works on bioinformatics algorithms and clinical informatics.
He has published more than 100 papers with over 60k citations and has been identified as a Top 1% Scholar Worldwide by Clarivate Analytics since 2019. He achieved 30 Under 30 Asia in Healthcare and Science, Top 10 Innovators Under 35 Asia Pacific by MITTR, and APEC Science Prize for Innovation, Research, and Education.
🔍 Recruitment: We are looking for dedicated and self-motivated students and postdocs with passion in bioinformatics. If you enjoy deciphering biological and medical big data by writing up your own code to target for the best speed, sensitivity and accuracy, you might be interested in working with me. Please contact me through email with your CV.
🌍 Visiting Scholars: We invite scholars at any career stage for short-term or long-term collaborative visits at HKU. We offer an engaging research environment, access to advanced facilities and technologies, and numerous professional development opportunities.
