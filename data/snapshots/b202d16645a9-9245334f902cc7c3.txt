# Professor Gong, Peng

PhD University of Waterloo
Chair Professor
Vice-President and Pro-Vice-Chancellor (Academic Development)
Email: penggong@hku.hk


Image
Peng Gong is professor of global sustainability at the University of Hong Kong. He received his degrees in computer cartography and remote sensing from Nanjing University and the University of Waterloo, Canada. He taught in Geomatics Engineering at the University of Calgary, Environmental Science, Policy and Management at UC Berkeley, State Key Lab of Remote Sensing Science in the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Earth System Science at Tsinghua University before.
His research group concentrated on the application of pattern recognition and AI methods to satellite remote sensing data, produced the first 30m, 10m, and 3m resolution global land cover maps in the world. He co-authored a paper in Nature, estimated when in each province of China nitrogen concentration exceeded its ecological safety boundaries in waters and proposed an economic estimation for different approaches to water quality restoration in China.
In environmental health, he developed a spatio-temporal analysis technique based on spherical surfaces to track the origin of the cross-continental spreading of the 2003~2006 avian influenza. He co-led 12 annual Lancet publications on health and climate change from 2015~2026, proposed strategies to reducing climate change risk to health and tracked global efforts. He also led or participated in preparation of multiple Lancet publications related to planetary health and healthy cities.
He served as a key expert in response to global change of the Ministry of Science and Technology, in smart city development and healthy development assessment of Chinese cities of the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development of the Chinese Government. Internationally, he served as an advisor to Future Earth. He is an Earth Commissioner, and is on the International Advisory Board of Lancet.
