## Lingpeng Kong
I am an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Hong Kong (HKU), and a co-director of the HKU NLP Lab. My work lies at the intersection of natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML), with a focus on representation learning, structured prediction, and generative models. Before joining HKU, I was a research scientist at DeepMind (London). I obtained my Ph.D.
from Carnegie Mellon University. For more details, see my academic c.v.
